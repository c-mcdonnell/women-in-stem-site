About the Show

Comedy that actually knows what a p-value is.

Women in STEM Comedy brings together female comedians who've worked in labs, written code, studied cells, and built infrastructure — and lived to make jokes about it.

Each show features original stand-up sets that draw from real STEM experiences: the impostor syndrome, the jargon, the conferences, the pipeline problem.

Hi, I'm Cassidy.

Host & Producer, Women in STEM Comedy

A former traffic engineer (it's a real thing), I work in software by day and spend the rest of my time writing, running, and performing standup.

I started Women in STEM as a one-off experiment. Several sold-out shows later, we've had hundreds of people come through the door and an outpouring of incredible feedback.
